    Sustained Attention to Response Task
    SART — Robertson et al., 1997
    Measure ability to withhold responses to infrequent targets during rapid rhythmic responding. 18 practice trials + 135 test trials (~4 min).
  • Ready
    Balloon Analogue Risk Task
    BART
    Measure risk-taking and impulsive decision-making. Pump a balloon to earn money — but pop it and you lose everything. 2 practice + 30 test balloons (~4 min).
  • Ready
    Multi-Object Tracking — Speed
    MOT Speed — Pylyshyn & Storm, 1988
    Track 4 targets among 8 identical moving circles. Speed adapts via 3-Down/1-Up staircase to measure your visual tracking limit. 8 practice + up to 30 test trials (~10 min).
  • Ready
    Multi-Object Tracking — Capacity
    MOT Capacity — Pylyshyn & Storm, 1988
    How many objects can you track at once? Target count adapts via 2-Down/1-Up staircase (1–6 targets among 10 circles). 8 practice + up to 40 test trials (~12 min).
